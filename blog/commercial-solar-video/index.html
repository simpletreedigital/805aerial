Behind the Scenes: Video for Commercial Solar

Solar and energy advisory brands need video that builds trust fast. Here's our work for Commercial Solar.

Published September 2026 · 805 Aerial · 5 min read

Solar and energy advisory companies operate in a category where trust is everything, business owners are being asked to make a significant capital decision, often based on unfamiliar technical and financing information. Commercial-Solar.org connects businesses with vetted commercial solar installers across the US and helps them compare zero-cap-ex financing before the remaining IRA tax credits expire, and our team produced video content to support their marketing.

What This Kind of Project Requires

Explainer and brand video for a solar advisory site needs to translate technical information (financing, incentives, installation process) into something a business owner can understand in under two minutes, without oversimplifying to the point of being misleading.

More Than Rooftop Panels

Commercial-Solar.org covers a wider range of project types than a typical residential site, including rooftop solar, carport solar, solar farms, solar plus storage, and even solar for AI data centers, a category that barely existed a few years ago.

A Nationwide Footprint

Their coverage spans states from California and Texas to New York, Florida, Georgia, Illinois, Ohio, Michigan, and more, reflecting how commercial solar financing has become a national conversation rather than a regional one.

Our Takeaway

Remote production work like this rounds out what we do day to day around San Luis Obispo County, and it's a category we've built real experience in.
