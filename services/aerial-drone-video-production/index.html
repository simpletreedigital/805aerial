What Aerial Drone Video Production Includes

Every project starts with a short call to understand the goal: sell a property faster, promote a destination, document a construction site, or build out a brand's marketing library. From there, 805 Aerial handles the full production process.

  • Pre-flight planning and airspace check. San Luis Obispo County includes controlled airspace near San Luis Obispo County Regional Airport and Paso Robles Municipal Airport. Every flight is checked against current FAA airspace authorizations before the drone launches.
  • On-site cinematography. Smooth, stabilized aerial movement, not the jerky footage produced by a consumer drone on auto-pilot modes. Shots are composed the way a cinematographer would compose a ground shot, using leading lines, elevation changes, and the Central Coast's natural topography.
  • Editing and color grading. Raw drone footage is edited into a finished video sized for its actual use, whether that's a 30-second social clip, a 90-second property tour, or a longer brand film.
  • Delivery in the formats you need. MLS-ready video, vertical cuts for Instagram and TikTok, and full-resolution masters for a client's own archive.

Who Uses Aerial Drone Video on the Central Coast

Real estate agents are the most common client, since an aerial establishing shot at the top of a listing video reliably increases watch time and inquiries. But aerial video has grown well beyond real estate. Paso Robles wineries use it to show the scale and setting of a vineyard in a way a tasting room photo cannot. Hospitality groups along Pismo Beach and Avila Beach use aerial footage in destination marketing. Contractors and developers use recurring aerial flights to document construction progress from a consistent vantage point over time.

Local note: the Central Coast's terrain, ocean bluffs, rolling vineyard hills, and dense coastal towns like Morro Bay, makes drone framing more technical than flat inland markets. A flight plan that works in the Salinas Valley often does not translate directly to a Morro Bay hillside shoot.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need my own permission to have a drone flown over my property?

If the flight stays within your own property boundaries and doesn't cross into restricted or controlled airspace, homeowner or business owner permission is generally all that's needed. Flights near beaches, parks, or airports may need an additional check, which we handle before scheduling.

Can you fly near the coast and beaches in San Luis Obispo County?

Yes, with planning. Some beach and coastal areas fall under state park or wildlife protection rules that limit drone use, particularly around sensitive habitat. We check the specific location before booking rather than assuming every beach is open airspace.

How is licensed drone video different from a hobbyist with a drone?

An FAA Part 107 remote pilot certificate means the pilot has passed a knowledge test covering airspace, weather, and regulations, and can legally operate a drone for commercial (paid) work. It also generally means better insurance coverage and more consistent adherence to no-fly zones.

What if it's foggy on the Central Coast the day of my shoot?

Marine layer fog is common along the coast in the morning. We build shoot windows around it, and can typically reschedule within the same week if conditions don't clear.

Do you provide raw footage or only edited video?

Both. Most clients receive an edited final video, but raw or lightly color-corrected footage is available on request for clients who want to edit in-house.
